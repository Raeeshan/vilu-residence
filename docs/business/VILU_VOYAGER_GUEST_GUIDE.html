6. Maamigili island information

Maamigili is a real, inhabited local island in South Ari Atoll — home to a working community, not a private resort. It sits inside the South Ari Marine Protected Area (SAMPA), one of a small number of places worldwide where researchers have documented year-long residency of individual whale sharks (Harvey-Carroll et al., Scientific Reports, 2021). Maamigili also has its own domestic airport — the only one in South Ari Atoll — which is why many South Ari-bound travelers, not just those staying in Maamigili, land here first.

Because it's a real village, you'll find local shops, cafés, a mosque, and normal island life alongside the beach and marine side of things. Nearby, you'll find a few local restaurants (including The Chef, The Beak, and The Coffee Concept), grocery shops, a mosque, and an ATM — ask our team for directions to any of these. Part of what makes a stay here different from a private-island resort is exactly this — genuine local-island life, not a bubble.

7. Bikini Beach & local dress

Maamigili has a designated Bikini Beach — swimwear is welcome there, on the boat, and once you're outside the inhabited island. Everywhere else on the island — the village, shops, common areas — please dress modestly: a T-shirt or blouse with shorts or trousers is fine for both men and women, in line with local Maldivian custom on inhabited islands. Our team can point you to Bikini Beach on arrival.

8. Forbidden items & the alcohol rule

Maldivian local (inhabited) islands, including Maamigili, do not permit alcohol under national law — this applies island-wide, not just at Vilu. Alcohol is only available on resort islands and liveaboard vessels elsewhere in the country. Alongside alcohol, drugs, dogs, and pork are also not permitted on Maamigili or any local inhabited island — please don't bring any of these with you.

Please note that Vilu Residence isn't responsible for delays or cancellations of these speedboats or ferries caused by circumstances beyond our control, such as bad weather.

10. Friday transport limitations

Friday is the main day of prayer and rest across the Maldives, including Maamigili — the direct Malé↔Maamigili speedboat does not run on Fridays. The alternative route via Dhigurah runs a shifted Friday schedule instead: Malé→Dhigurah at 10:00 & 16:00 (versus 11:00 & 16:00 on other days), and Dhigurah→Malé at 13:30 (versus 13:00 on other days). If your own transfer date falls on a Friday, confirm your exact timing with us in advance — schedules can still change.

11. The airport

Villa International Airport (VAM) is Maamigili's own airport — the only one in South Ari Atoll, and a genuine alternative to arriving by speedboat. It's a short, easy trip from the guesthouse. Domestic flights connect from Malé, roughly 20–30 minutes in the air, with 3–5 flights daily, mainly operated by Flyme (Villa Air). Domestic flights are booked and paid for separately from your Vilu package — see Section 9. 14. Marine safety, wildlife & external activities

Please always follow your guide's instructions in the water. Keep a respectful distance from whale sharks, manta rays, and other marine life — no touching, no chasing, no blocking an animal's path. These are wild animals in a protected marine area, and following guide instructions protects both you and them. Please also respect the coral reefs and the wider marine environment — any damage may result in additional charges or fines.

Sightings of whale sharks, manta rays, and other marine life are never guaranteed on any specific excursion — this is true everywhere in the Maldives, not just at Vilu.

Booking activities with outside operators: if you're considering an excursion offered by someone outside Vilu Residence, please be cautious — these individuals may not hold the licenses required to operate safely with tourists. If something goes wrong on an unlicensed trip, our team may still end up involved in resolving it, so we'd always recommend a licensed, reputable provider instead. If you do choose to book externally, please let us know in advance — we'll ask you to sign a liability waiver first. We're always happy to help arrange safe, enjoyable activities directly instead.

15. Non-swimmers

Let us know in advance if you or anyone in your group is not a confident swimmer. Life jackets are available for snorkeling excursions, and our team and excursion guides can adjust the experience so everyone can safely take part.

16. Weather

The Maldives has a tropical climate with a wetter monsoon season and a drier season across the year; short showers can happen even in the drier months, and conditions can affect whether a boat excursion goes ahead on a given day. If weather affects a planned excursion, our team will help you reschedule where possible.

17. Payments

When you book directly with us, we never take a deposit, prepayment, or card details online — your reservation is simply confirmed, and payment is settled once you're here.

All prices at Vilu Residence are listed in US Dollars (USD). Once you're staying with us, you can settle your bill in USD or Maldivian Rufiyaa (MVR) — MVR payments are converted at our hotel exchange rate of 1 USD = 20 MVR, and please note MVR payments can't be converted back to USD, so we'd recommend settling in USD for the best value. Card payments are also accepted, subject to a 3.5% bank processing fee.

Our restaurant menu (for room service or dining) is priced separately in MVR — see Section 4 for how to arrange this.
