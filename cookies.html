Cookies & Technology

What we use to run this website, and the choices you have over the optional parts.

Last updated: 3 September 2026

We use a small number of cookies and similar browser storage to run our website and, only with your permission, to understand how it's used. You choose which optional categories to allow — nothing optional loads or runs until you say yes. You can change your choice at any time using "Privacy Choices" in our website footer, or the button below.

Essential — Always on

These are required for the website to work and cannot be turned off. They include: checking room availability, submitting a booking, remembering your language choice, and remembering the cookie preference you've made here.

Preferences — Optional

Reserved for remembering optional choices you make on the site. Our use of this category is currently limited — most of the site's functionality today is covered by the Essential category above.

Analytics — Optional, off until you consent

Google Analytics (GA4), used to understand how visitors use our site — for example, which pages are viewed and which links are used.

We use Google's Basic Consent Mode: the Google Analytics script itself is not requested from Google's servers, and no analytics measurement is sent, until you actively accept this category. Nothing that happens on the site before you consent is recorded or sent later — activity from before you consent is not queued or replayed once you accept.

If you later change your mind and withdraw consent, future analytics measurement stops immediately. Cookies that Google Analytics already placed in your browser while Analytics was active may remain until they expire on their own or you remove them yourself through your browser settings — withdrawing consent stops new measurement, but it does not reach back into your browser to delete cookies that are already there.

Marketing — Optional, currently inactive

Reserved for possible future use, such as advertising or remarketing tools. No marketing or advertising technology is active on this website today. Turning this category on currently has no effect, but the consent system is built so that nothing marketing-related will run without your permission once — or if — it is introduced.

Google Maps

Our website includes a Google Maps embed showing our location, loaded only as you scroll to it. The map is provided by Google and may process technical information when it loads. We currently treat the map as a functional website feature and do not use it for our own advertising or analytics purposes, and it works regardless of your cookie choices above.

Fonts

The typefaces used on this website are hosted directly by Vilu Residence rather than loaded from Google's servers, so viewing this website does not create a connection to any external font provider.

Questions

For anything else about how we handle information, see our Privacy Policy, or contact us at Viluresidence@gmail.com.
