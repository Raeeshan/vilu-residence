Your Trip, Planned as One

Vilu Residence, and everything around it

Every package below is built around Vilu Residence — our guesthouse on Maamigili, right in the South Ari Atoll's whale shark grounds. Your room, your transfers, and the snorkeling, cruises, and island trips that make this atoll worth visiting are planned as one trip, not booked separately.

Every journey includes round-trip transfer, your stay at Vilu Residence, daily breakfast, and snorkeling with whale sharks, manta rays and turtles.

Frequently asked questions

Is the round-trip transfer really included?
Yes — every package on this page includes round-trip speedboat transfer between Malé and Maamigili, already included in the price shown.
What accommodation is included?
Every package includes your stay at Vilu Residence in Maamigili — see the exact accommodation line on the package you're interested in above for the specifics of that package.
Can I book accommodation without choosing a holiday package?
Yes — if you'd prefer to arrange your activities separately, you can check Vilu Residence's live accommodation availability through our booking engine. Breakfast is included with your stay either way.
Do I need to pay a deposit to enquire, or to hold a package?
No. Enquiring is free and doesn't commit you to anything. We don't collect deposits, prepayment, or card details online for direct bookings — payment is made in person, in cash (USD or EUR), during your stay.
What if I need to change my dates or cancel?
Our published cancellation policy for direct bookings (website, WhatsApp, email, or phone) is free cancellation, with no deposit ever collected. If you book through a third-party platform instead, that platform's own cancellation policy applies.
How quickly will I hear back after I enquire?
Within 24 hours.
What if the weather isn't right for one of the activities?
Weather and sea conditions can affect any of these trips. Vilu checks conditions ahead of each excursion, and a trip may be postponed, changed, or cancelled if conditions aren't suitable — we'll let you know as early as possible.
