South Ari Atoll vs Other Maldives Regions

Each Maldives atoll has a different character — here's how South Ari compares to North Malé and Baa.

The Maldives' 26 atolls aren't interchangeable — each region has a different character, and it's worth choosing based on what you actually want from the trip rather than just proximity to Malé.

North Malé Atoll

The closest atoll cluster to Velana International Airport, so transfer times are short — often under an hour. It has the highest concentration of resorts and some well-known surf breaks on its eastern reefs. Being close to the capital also means it's the most heavily visited region.

Baa Atoll

Designated a UNESCO Biosphere Reserve, Baa Atoll is best known for Hanifaru Bay, one of the world's best-documented manta ray feeding aggregation sites — though access is seasonal and tightly regulated to protect the reserve.

South Ari Atoll

Further from Malé (a genuine 1h45m by speedboat, or 20 minutes by air), which is the trade-off for what it offers: year-round whale sharks, regular manta sightings, and a mix of resorts and long-established local islands like Maamigili and Dhigurah, making it accessible to guesthouse travelers as well as resort guests.

Choosing a region

If minimizing transfer time matters most, North Malé is hard to beat. If a specific, tightly seasonal wildlife event is the goal, Baa Atoll's Hanifaru Bay is worth planning around.
