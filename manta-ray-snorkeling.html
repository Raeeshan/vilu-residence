Manta Ray Snorkeling in the Maldives

Manta rays are a regular, year-round sight around South Ari Atoll's cleaning stations — glide alongside them just off Maamigili's reef.

What the excursion involves

Snorkel with manta rays gliding through the reef near Maamigili. Equipment is provided and excursions typically depart at 09:00 after breakfast, same as our whale shark trips.

Snorkelers welcome

Where and when

Manta rays are also a regular sight in South Ari, with sightings possible throughout the year at several cleaning stations around the atoll — some sites are more active earlier in the year, others later, so exact timing is best confirmed locally when you book.

What to expect at a cleaning station

At a cleaning station, manta rays hover in place while small reef fish clean parasites from their skin and gills — which is what makes these sites so reliable for sightings compared to open-water encounters. Because South Ari's stations sit close to shore and close to the surface, they're snorkel-accessible, not dive-only.
