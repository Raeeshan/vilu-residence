Things to Do in Maamigili

Every kind of excursion available while staying in Maamigili — and how to build them into your holiday.

Staying in Maamigili puts you within reach of well over a dozen different excursions: snorkeling with whale sharks and manta rays, reef and turtle snorkeling, sandbank and island trips, dolphin and sunset cruises, several styles of fishing, and a handful of quieter experiences. Guests can combine several experiences during their stay, and our holiday packages are designed to make it easier to plan accommodation and selected activities together. This guide walks through what's actually on offer, what each experience is, and where to go for the fuller detail on the two most in-demand excursions.

Quick Overview — What's Available in Maamigili

There are many ways to experience Maamigili and the surrounding South Ari Atoll during a stay at Vilu Residence, from marine-life snorkeling and reef trips to cruises, fishing and quieter beach experiences.

Water & Snorkeling

Whale Shark Snorkeling, Manta Ray Snorkeling, Turtle Snorkeling, Nurse Shark Snorkeling, Reef Snorkeling (Fenfushi, Dhiddhoo & Ariyadhoo)

Boat & Island Trips

Sandbank Escape, Picnic Island Experience, Dolphin Cruise, Sunset Cruise

Fishing

Night Fishing Experience, Big Game Fishing, Octopus Hunting Experience, Lobster Hunting Experience

Beyond Vilu

Diving, arranged through an appropriately certified dive centre — not operated by Vilu directly

Quieter Touches

Professional Photography, Romantic Beach Dinner, Cinema at the Beach

Whale Shark Snorkeling

South Ari Atoll is known for year-round whale shark encounters, and Maamigili provides a practical base for reaching the nearby search areas. The excursion is generally accessible to snorkelers rather than requiring a diving certification, and sightings are never guaranteed. For the full detail — what the excursion involves, group sizes, what's included, and what happens if a shark isn't found that day — see our dedicated Whale Shark Snorkeling guide.

Read our full Whale Shark Snorkeling guide →

Manta Ray Snorkeling

South Ari Atoll's manta rays are year-round residents of Maldivian waters, but rather than one fixed site, our guides check recent sightings and sea conditions and choose where to search from there. Like the whale shark excursion, it's generally accessible to snorkelers, and sightings are never guaranteed. For the full detail on what to expect, see our dedicated Manta Ray Snorkeling guide.

Read our full Manta Ray Snorkeling guide →

Reef, Turtle & Nurse Shark Snorkeling

A few reef-based excursions are also available beyond the two flagship trips.

Turtle Snorkeling

A separate excursion for snorkeling with turtles.

Nurse Shark Snorkeling

A separate excursion for snorkeling with nurse sharks — a different species from whale sharks.

Reef Snorkeling — Fenfushi, Dhiddhoo & Ariyadhoo

Vilu also arranges reef snorkeling trips to Fenfushi, Dhiddhoo, and Ariyadhoo, as options beyond Maamigili's own house reef.

Sandbank Escape & Picnic Island Experience

Sandbank Escape

A boat trip out to a sandbank — an area of open sand with no trees or island vegetation — for time away from the island itself.

Picnic Island Experience

A separate island excursion, distinct from the Sandbank Escape.

Dolphin & Sunset Cruises

Dolphin Cruise

A boat cruise in the waters around South Ari Atoll in search of dolphins.

Sunset Cruise

A boat cruise timed for sunset, watching the sky change color over the water as the day ends.

Fishing Experiences

Maamigili offers several distinct styles of fishing trip.

Night Fishing Experience

An evening fishing excursion after dark.

Big Game Fishing

A fishing trip further into open water, for guests interested in sport fishing.

Octopus Hunting Experience

A guided octopus-hunting excursion along the reef flats.

Lobster Hunting Experience

A guided lobster-hunting excursion along the reef.

Diving

Maamigili and the surrounding reefs are also a base for diving, though Vilu Residence itself doesn't operate dive trips — diving here is arranged through an appropriately certified dive centre, not as part of a Vilu excursion. Weather & Trip Changes

Weather and sea conditions can affect these excursions, and trips may be changed, postponed, or cancelled when conditions aren't suitable. Availability can also depend on scheduling and the activity itself. Vilu checks conditions ahead of each excursion and will let you know of any changes as early as possible.

Frequently Asked Questions

What's the difference between the Whale Shark and Manta Ray excursions?
Both are snorkeling excursions in the waters around South Ari Atoll, but they involve different animals with different behavior. See our dedicated guides for each for the full detail.
Are these activities included in a holiday package, or booked separately?
Our holiday packages combine accommodation with selected experiences. Which activities are included depends on the package and length of stay — see our Holiday Packages page or contact us directly for current options.
Do I need to be a certified diver for any of these?
No — every excursion listed on this page is snorkeling, boat, or fishing based, and doesn't require a diving certification. Diving itself is available separately in South Ari Atoll through an appropriately certified dive centre, not as a Vilu excursion.
What if the weather isn't right for an excursion?
Weather and sea conditions can affect any of these trips. Vilu checks conditions ahead of each excursion, and a trip may be postponed, changed, or cancelled if conditions aren't suitable — we'll let you know as early as possible.
Is turtle snorkeling the same trip as whale shark or manta ray snorkeling?
No — it's a separate excursion.
Can I combine several of these activities during one stay?
Yes — guests can combine several experiences during their stay, and our holiday packages are designed to make it easier to plan accommodation and selected activities together.
Does Vilu Residence operate diving trips itself?
No. Vilu doesn't run diving directly — it's arranged through an appropriately certified dive centre in South Ari Atoll.
Where can I find activity and excursion options?
Our Holiday Packages combine accommodation with selected experiences and are the best place to start when planning your stay. See our current Holiday Packages or contact Vilu Residence and we'll help you choose the option that suits your trip.
