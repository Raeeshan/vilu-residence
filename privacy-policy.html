Who we are

Vilu Residence is a guesthouse in Maamigili, Alifu Dhaalu Atoll, Republic of Maldives, operated by Mexiczone. This policy explains what information we collect through viluresidence.net, why we collect it, and the choices you have.

If you have any question about this policy or about your information, contact us at Viluresidence@gmail.com.

Information we collect

Direct website booking

When you book a room directly on our website, we collect your name, email address, phone number, country, chosen room(s) and dates, and number of guests, so we can process and manage your reservation.

Enquiry form

Our website's enquiry form does not submit your details to our servers. It opens your own email application with your message pre-filled, addressed to us. We only receive your enquiry if and when you choose to send that email yourself.

WhatsApp

If you message us on WhatsApp, that conversation takes place within WhatsApp and is governed by WhatsApp's own privacy terms, not by this website.

Email

If you email us directly, or your enquiry-form email reaches us, we use the information in that email to respond to you and manage any related booking.

Analytics

With your consent (see "Cookies and analytics" below), we use Google Analytics to understand how visitors use our website — for example, which pages are viewed and which links are used. We do not use analytics to identify you personally, and it does not run until you have given your consent.

How we use your information

We use the information described above to:

  • respond to booking requests and enquiries;
  • create, manage, and communicate with you about a reservation;
  • operate and maintain our website and booking system; and
  • only with your consent, understand how our website is used so we can improve it.

We do not currently use your information for advertising or remarketing.

Sharing your information

We do not sell your personal information. We may share information only where reasonably necessary to:

  • provide the accommodation or travel-related service you have requested;
  • operate the service providers our website and booking system depend on (see "Service providers and Google services" below);
  • meet a legal obligation; or
  • act on your specific request to arrange a service with a third party, such as an excursion or transfer operator.

We do not share your information with any other party for their own independent marketing purposes.

Service providers and Google services

Our website and booking system are built on Google Firebase, which stores and processes your reservation information on our behalf. If you consent to analytics, we also use Google Analytics. These providers act on our instructions and are bound by their own data-processing terms.

International processing

Some of the services we rely on, including Google Firebase and Google Analytics, are provided by Google, whose infrastructure may process information in multiple countries where Google or its service providers operate.

Cookies and analytics

Our website uses cookies and similar technologies as described in our Cookies & Technology page, including a consent system that keeps optional analytics switched off until you actively agree to it.

How long we keep information

We keep personal information only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes for which it was collected and to meet applicable business, accounting and legal requirements. Reservation and transaction records may need to be retained for longer periods where required for accounting or tax purposes. When personal information is no longer reasonably required, we aim to delete or minimize it where practical and legally permitted.

Your choices

You can accept, reject, or customize non-essential cookies at any time using "Privacy Choices" in our website footer. You can also ask us what information we hold about a reservation or enquiry, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete it once it is no longer needed for the purposes described in this policy, by emailing Viluresidence@gmail.com.

Security

We use industry-standard tools, including access-controlled Google Firebase security rules, to help protect the information we hold. No method of storing or transmitting information online is completely secure, but we take reasonable steps to protect it.

Children

Vilu Residence is a hospitality booking website intended for adults arranging travel. We do not knowingly collect information directly from children.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time as our website or our practices change. The "Last updated" date at the top of this page reflects the most recent revision.
